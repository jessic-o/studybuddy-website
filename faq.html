FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the device, app plans, campus use, and how StudyBuddy compares to app-only blockers.

What is StudyBuddy?

StudyBuddy is a physical phone-locking device paired with a campus productivity app. It helps students reduce distractions, plan study time, and connect with classmates.

How does the device work?

You place your phone in the device for a study session you choose. The phone stays locked during that block. When it’s break time, the device vibrates so you know you can step away.

How is this different from Opal, Forest, or Freedom?

Those tools are software-based blockers. StudyBuddy adds a physical lock and vibration reminder so focus isn’t only an on-screen setting — it’s a tangible habit cue.

Is the app free?

Yes. Free includes a study planner, focus mode, campus feed, and study group chats. Premium is $5.99/month with a 7-day free trial. Pro adds custom guides, practice exams, and schedules.

Where can I buy the device?

Our go-to-market plan includes Target, Walmart, and Best Buy, plus direct checkout on this site for the class demo.

Do I need the device to use the app?

You can use free app features on their own. The full StudyBuddy experience pairs the device lock with the app’s focus session for the strongest distraction-free setup.

What are tokens?

The more you study, the more tokens you earn. Tokens can unlock seasonal themes and extras inside the app.

How do campus partnerships work?

We offer demonstrations and student discounts with colleges, student organizations, and campus bookstores. Contact us to set one up.
