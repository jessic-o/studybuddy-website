Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy describes how StudyBuddy would handle information in a production product. This website is a class project demo.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use StudyBuddy, we may collect account details (name, email, university, major, classes), study activity (session length, streaks, goals), uploaded study materials on Premium/Pro plans, and basic device/app diagnostics.

How we use information

  • Provide focus sessions, planners, campus feed, and group chat features
  • Generate quizzes, flashcards, and study recommendations on paid plans
  • Improve product performance and fix bugs
  • Communicate about orders, subscriptions, and campus partnerships

Sharing

We do not sell student personal information. We may share data with service providers who help host the app, process payments, or support campus partnerships — only as needed to operate StudyBuddy.

Student content

Notes, PDFs, and recordings you upload remain yours. We use them to power Premium features you request and do not publish them to the campus feed without your action.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by contacting us. You can cancel subscriptions anytime according to our business policies.

Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@studybuddy.demo or use our Contact page.
