About Us

Built by students, for students who want their time back

StudyBuddy started as a simple idea: make focus physical. Put the phone away, feel the lock click, and get back to the work that matters.

Introduction

StudyBuddy is entering the education technology market with a focus on student productivity. We help college students reduce phone distractions and develop better studying habits through a paired device and app experience.

Unlike app-only blockers such as Opal, Forest, Freedom, or a basic Pomodoro timer, StudyBuddy adds a physical lock that turns intention into action — a real reminder that it’s time to concentrate.

Mission

Stay focused. Build better habits.

Help students stay focused, build better study habits, and spend less time distracted by their phones.

Value

Make studying manageable

Encourage putting distractions aside, taking intentional breaks, and making the most of every study block.

Outreach

Community on campus

We partner with colleges, student organizations, and campus bookstores — with demos and student discounts that make trying StudyBuddy easy.
