Business Policies

Returns, subscriptions, shipping, and student discounts — written for our class project business plan.

Device returns

Unopened devices may be returned within 30 days of purchase with proof of receipt. Opened devices in working condition may be eligible for store credit within 14 days. Defective units are covered by a 1-year limited warranty.

Subscriptions

Premium includes a 7-day free trial. Plans renew monthly or yearly until canceled. Cancel anytime in-app; access continues through the paid period. No partial refunds on the current billing cycle except where required by law.

Shipping & retail

Devices sold via Target, Walmart, and Best Buy follow each retailer’s shipping and pickup policies. Direct demo orders on this site are illustrative only and do not ship real hardware.

Student discounts

Verified students and partner organizations may receive promotional pricing on devices or first-month Premium. Abuse of discount codes may result in removal of the offer.

Acceptable use

Campus feed and group chats must follow community guidelines: no harassment, academic dishonesty facilitation, or illegal content. We may suspend accounts that violate these rules.

Pricing changes

We may update plan prices with notice to subscribers. Existing billing periods are honored at the rate charged for that period.

Questions about these policies? Visit Contact Us or read our Privacy Policy.
